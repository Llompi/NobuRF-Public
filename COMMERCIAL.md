# Commercial licenses

NobuRF is free software under the [GNU AGPL-3.0](LICENSE), and the complete solver is in this repository. A
commercial license is the same software under different terms: it lets you build NobuRF into closed software
without the AGPL's source-sharing requirements. There is no paid edition and no feature held back.

## You don't need one if

- You use NobuRF to design and simulate your own antennas and boards, at work or at home. The designs and results
  are yours either way (see [Your designs are yours](README.md#your-designs-are-yours)).
- You run NobuRF unchanged on your own machines or servers, including from your own scripts through the `noburf`
  command-line tool, and don't ship it to anyone else.
- You change NobuRF and share your changes under the AGPL, including with the users of any service you run it in.

## You probably need one if

- **You build NobuRF into a product.** For example, a PCB or EDA tool that ships the solver inside it, or a
  component maker's "simulate our part" page that runs NobuRF in visitors' browsers, and you don't want to
  release that product's source under the AGPL.
- **You run a changed NobuRF as a hosted service** and don't want to offer your changes to its users under the AGPL.
- **Your company doesn't allow AGPL software.** Many defense, aerospace and telecom companies keep AGPL code off
  their approved-software lists, even for internal use. A commercial license gives your legal team a standard
  agreement to approve instead.

## Sponsored work and support

If your team relies on NobuRF, you can pay to have a solver feature, file format, integration or validation case
prioritized, or for support. Sponsored work is merged into the public AGPL version, so everyone gets it. This
doesn't require a commercial license.
