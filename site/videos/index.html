Built for the Kimi K3.1 Pro hackathon on Cerebras.

It is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by any commercial solver vendor.

NobuRF is free software under the GNU AGPL-3.0. The designs and results you make with it are yours. To build it into closed software, see commercial licenses.

Privacy: NobuRF's solver runs in your browser, on your GPU or CPU, and this site never uploads your designs. The app keeps your latest design in this browser's storage. NobuRF has no ads, sets no cookies of its own, and doesn't track you across sites. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which uses its cookie-free Web Analytics to count visits and measure page load times. If Cloudflare's bot protection flags a visit, it may set a security cookie. Fonts load from Google Fonts. Cloudflare and Google receive standard request details, such as your IP address. The AI design features aren't part of this site. They run from your own computer with your own API key, and they send your prompts to the AI provider you choose.
